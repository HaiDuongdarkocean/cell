Quiet Confidence

Cell is a learning companion, not a loud tool. The foundation is designed to feel calm, focused, and trustworthy while still encouraging curiosity.

Overview

Calm, Focused, Learnable, Elegant — four principles that guide every token and component.

Calm

No aggressive color, no heavy shadow. Restrained use of accent.

Focused

Single accent color, clear hierarchy, purposeful motion.

Learnable

Consistent patterns: 5 type roles, 4px spacing, concentric radius.

Elegant

Warm off-white light theme. Near-black with faint blue tint dark theme.

Neutral-first, single indigo accent as punctuation. Color is organized by role, not by hex.
